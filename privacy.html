Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Recap Uploader is a personal desktop tool run by its owner on their own computer. It has no website, no user accounts and no server.

What the tool stores

When the owner logs in to a social media account, the platform gives the tool an access token. The token is saved only on the owner's own computer, in a local file. It is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own account.

What the tool does not do

It does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person. It does not use analytics, cookies or advertising. No data is sent to anyone except the platform the owner logs in to.

Deleting data

The owner can delete the token files at any time, and can remove the tool's access in the settings of their social media account.
